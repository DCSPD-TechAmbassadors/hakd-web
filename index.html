HakD 2025:

To the Stars

Welcome to HakD!

HakD is an entirely student-led event of the year for young tech enthusiasts, innovators and creators.

Open to all students from Year 6 (Grade 5) and above, HakD is your chance to unleash your creativity, dive into STEAM and tackle real-world challenges in an exciting, collaborative environment.

No experience? No problem! HakD is designed for everyone, whether you’re a coding pro or just getting started. All you need is your passion for technology and a willingness to learn.

What do I do at HakD?

1 - Register

Use the link or QR code at the bottom of this page to sign up. When you arrive at the event, you will receive a swag bag full of HakD themed goodies! We will group your pair with another pair from a different school to form a team of 4 students.

2 - Workshops

On the first day of the event, you will participate in tech-related workshops! You will join the workshops and learn new skills like coding robots, hacking and building robots. If you're feeling tactical, you can coordinate which workshops to attend as a group so you can combine the knowledge from all the workshops in the next step!

3 - Projects

You will also be given a set of scenarios based on our theme of Need for Speed, which will be revealed during the Opening Ceremony. As a team, you will pick a scenario and work on solving it through technology. Your prototype can involve software, hardware, or both!

4 - Present

At the end of the event, you will get the opportunity to present your products in a science fair style presentation. Good luck!

What to bring?

We've got you covered with:

  • Robotics kits
  • 3D printers

  • And more…

  • Laptop and charger
  • Pocket money for snacks

  • Your passion for technology
  • Any other tools you think you'll need
